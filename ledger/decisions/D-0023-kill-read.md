**Decision:** the improvement room D-0002 (track and task) names stands: with test access, `muse-spark-1.3` reaches exact on 0 of 9 T tests of `mealybug:palette-scroll-bg` in 15 minutes of model wall time, below the threshold of 7 (SPEC §5 S1, "Kill read"). The headline stays as SPEC §3 states it; the cost-to-pass headline is not taken.
**Not this:** reading room as thin and switching the headline to cost to pass (steps, tokens and minutes per family); treating this reading as an attempt or a curve point, which SPEC §5 S1 forbids.
**Warrant:** the reading of 2026-11-07 in `evidence/kill-read/summary.json`: baseline exact 0 of 9, mean pixel match 0.7510 over the 9 ROMs; final exact 0 of 9, mean pixel match 0.8545 (the round-6 core, kept best of 12 scored rounds); 12 completed calls, 286,001 prompt and 153,798 completion tokens, 900,000 ms of model wall time, with a 13th call cut by the hard stop. Each round's reading is in `evidence/kill-read/rounds.jsonl`, every call's messages and reply in `evidence/kill-read/calls.jsonl` and `evidence/kill-read/telemetry/llm.jsonl`, the final diff in `evidence/kill-read/final.patch`. The driver is `evidence/kill-read/drive.ts`: `runApiLoop` from `scripts/api-loop.ts` (D-0016, the repairer API loop), one round per call, `reasoning_effort` low, keep-best between rounds, scored by `tools/replay.mjs` on `evidence/kill-read/roms.json` (T's list from `splits.json` only; no H or V reading entered the directory). The final core passes the base suites, blargg 15/15, mooneye:mbc1 12/12, dmg-acid2 1/1 (`evidence/kill-read/base-suites.txt`); it is evidence only and merges nowhere. False if: a repeat of this read under the same driver, model and budget reaches exact on 7 or more of the 9 T tests.
