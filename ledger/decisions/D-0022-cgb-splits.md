---
id: D-0022
title: The CGB tier's base, T, H and V families in splits-cgb.json, chosen by which CGB references the ROM pack holds
status: admitted
supersedes: null
---
**Decision:** `splits-cgb.json` holds five families, each marked `model: "CGB"`: base `cgb:acid2` (cgb-acid2); T `cgb:mealybug:palette-scroll-bg` (the palette, scroll and BG-control Mealybug ROMs of `splits.json`'s T, plus their CGB-only `*2` variants); H `cgb:acid-hell` (cgb-acid-hell) and `cgb:mealybug:window` (the window Mealybug ROMs of `splits.json`'s H, plus their `*2` variants); V `cgb:mealybug:wx-obj` (the wx and OBJ Mealybug ROMs of `splits.json`'s V). A Mealybug ROM scores against its `_cgb_c.png` (CPU CGB C, the revision `oracle/frontend.py` MODEL_OPTION runs SameBoy as); cgb-acid2 and cgb-acid-hell score against `<stem>.png`. A ROM with no such reference is excluded with its reason, and `splits-cgb.json`'s `excluded` equals `computeExcluded` over the fetched ROMs (suites/splits.test.ts). V-cgb opens only to a token from `runs/RUN-0002/vread.lock` (suites/score.ts guardV, CGB_RUN_ID; suites/score.test.ts). Its list freezes when `splits-cgb.json` first merges (D-0019).
**Not this:** the `_cgb_d.png` references, which every `*2` ROM lacks, so a CGB D tier scores 7 fewer T and H ROMs; Blargg `cgb_sound` or Mooneye ROMs in the tier, whose pass rules carry no CGB model-suffix rule (suites/splits.ts exclusionReason).
**Warrant:** a family is scorable only where a hardware reference exists. Reading of game-boy-test-roms v7.0 on 2026-11-07: Mealybug `ppu/` holds `_cgb_c.png` for every T ROM, for every H ROM except `m3_lcdc_win_en_change_multiple_wx` and `win_without_bg`, and for every V ROM except `m3_wx_4_change`, `m3_wx_5_change` and `m3_wx_6_change`; those five are excluded. Scored counts: base 1, T 14, H 1 + 8, V 5 ROMs (suites/splits.test.ts; V's count from `computeExcluded`, no V-cgb ROM run). False if: a scored ROM of `splits-cgb.json` has no reference file, or a V-cgb ROM is scored on CGB without the RUN-0002 token.
**Revisit when:** {when: "the CGB core fails cgb-acid2 after its bootstrap rounds by 15:00", then: "follow D-0019's revisit: show the tier as built-but-unrun and do not open RUN-0002"}
**Residue:** V-cgb's five ROMs are files that `splits.json`'s V also reaches (`mealybug:wx-obj`), so V and V-cgb are not independent: a RUN-0001 V read on DMG runs the same ROM files on other hardware. The oracle disagrees with four CGB C references: on 2026-11-07, sampling every 10th frame to 400 under align "frame", SameBoy 1.0.5 ea667ad as CPU CGB C matched every base, T and H reference exactly except `m3_lcdc_tile_sel_change` (8 px), `m3_lcdc_tile_sel_change2` (186 px), `m3_lcdc_tile_sel_win_change` (8 px) and `m3_lcdc_tile_sel_win_change2` (179 px); on those four, an oracle frame and the reference disagree, and the reference is the pass rule. The bundled `tools/replay.mjs` compiles in `splits.json`'s V globs only (lockstep/build-replay.ts), so a scratch repo without `splits-cgb.json` refuses V-cgb through those shared files alone.
